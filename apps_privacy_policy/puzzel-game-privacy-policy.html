Privacy Policy — Puzzle Game

Effective date: October 21, 2026  |  App: Puzzle Game (package name com.puzzlegame.match3) on Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or playing Puzzle Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the game.

Short version: We collect no personal information ourselves. Gameplay progress (scores, levels, sound settings) is stored only on your device. The game shows ads served by Unity Ads, which may use your advertising ID and device data to deliver and measure ads.

1. Information We Collect

  • Locally stored game data (never leaves your device): current level, best score, total score, sound/music settings. This is saved with standard device storage (PlayerPrefs) and is deleted if you clear the app data or uninstall.
  • Advertising identifiers and device data (collected by Unity Ads, not by us): when ads are shown, the Unity Ads SDK may process your advertising ID (AAID/GAID), device model, OS version, language, approximate region (country/city level), IP address, and interaction data with ads, to serve ads, prevent fraud, measure performance, and pay ad revenue.
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone data. The game has no login/account system.

2. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game integrates the Unity Ads monetization network (Game ID 800405865). Ads shown include:

  • Banner ads — only on the menu screens, never during gameplay.
  • Interstitial ads — shown only at natural break points, for example after a level is completed or after the round ends. Gameplay is always paused/finished when an interstitial appears.
  • Rewarded video ads — completely optional and only shown when you decide to watch one (for example “Watch ad to win +30 seconds”). Rewards are granted only when the video is watched to the end.

Unity Ads may show personalized advertising based on your advertising ID where permitted, or non-personalized advertising otherwise. In regions of the EEA/UK and other areas with consent requirements, Unity Ads shows its own consent dialog before targeted advertising; you can change or withdraw your choice through that system or through your device settings.

More information: Unity Privacy Policy and Unity Ads & cookies.

3. How Your Data Is Used and Shared

  • Ads data is used by Unity Ads to select ads, cap ad frequency, prevent ad fraud, and measure ad performance — that is how the game stays free.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Ad identifiers may be shared with Unity Ads (the ad network acting as a processor) and ad advertisers that receive ad delivery information through the network.

4. Children and Families

The puzzle content is family friendly. If Google Play classifies the game as target-audience restricted (families/children), ads will be configured accordingly (contextual/non-personalized, COPPA-compliant) as required by the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Reset / opt out of ads personalization: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of ads personalization.
  • Clear game data: device Settings → Apps → Puzzle Game → Storage → Clear data (this also removes saved scores/settings).
  • GDPR / CCPA rights: you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal data, and object to processing. Since we do not store personal data on our side, most requests are handled via the controls above or by contacting us (see below) — we will forward requests to Unity Ads if needed.

6. Data Security & Retention

Game data is stored on your device using platform-standard mechanisms. Advertising data is retained by Unity Ads according to their retention schedule (see Unity’s privacy policy). We keep no server-side copies of your gameplay data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game or its advertising features change. The “effective date” above will always show the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the app listing description.
