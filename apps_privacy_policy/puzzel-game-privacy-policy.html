Puzzle Game – Privacy Policy

App: Puzzle Game (package: com.robosimunomy.puzzlegame)  |  Developer: RoboSimunomy
Effective date: October 21, 2026  |  Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Puzzle Game Android app ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information, which device permissions it uses and why, and how advertising works in the Game. By installing or playing the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.

Short summary
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Level, scores and sound settings are stored only on your device.
  • No account or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.
  • The Game shows ads (Unity Ads). To show ads, Unity and its partners may collect your advertising ID and some device and usage data. See Section 4. In some regions you will be asked for your consent first.
  • Permissions: only Internet / network state / advertising ID, used for ads. No camera, microphone, storage or location access.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. About the Game

Puzzle Game is a free match-3 style puzzle game for Android. It is supported by advertising. The Game has no login, no online multiplayer and no server of ours that receives your data.

2. Information We Collect and Process

2.1 What we do NOT collect

We do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone data.

2.2 Information stored on your device only

DataWhat happens to it
Current level, best score, total scoreSaved in the Game's private storage on your phone (Unity PlayerPrefs) so you can continue where you stopped. Never uploaded.
Sound and music settingsSaved in the Game's private storage on your phone. Used only to remember your choices.

This data is removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

2.3 Information collected by our advertising partner

Because the Game shows ads, Unity Ads (a third-party service) may collect and process certain data from your device when ads are requested and shown. This is described in Section 4. We do not receive this data ourselves.

4. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Game uses the Unity Ads network (Game ID 800405865) to show the following ads:

  • Banner ads – shown only on menu screens, never during gameplay.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads – shown only at natural break points, for example after a level is completed or a round ends. Gameplay is always paused or finished when one appears.
  • Rewarded video ads – completely optional and shown only when you choose to watch one (for example "Watch ad to win +30 seconds"). The reward is given only when the video is watched to the end.
  • Data Unity and its partners may collect: your advertising ID (AAID/GAID), IP address (which gives an approximate location at country/city level), device model, operating-system version, language, app information, ad interactions (views and taps), and diagnostic or performance data. They use this to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and abuse, and pay ad revenue.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads: depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID, or non-personalised.
  • Your consent choices: in regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK), Unity Ads shows its own consent dialog before targeted advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice through that system or through your device settings.
  • Control of your advertising ID: on most Android phones you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Phone Settings → Google → Ads (the path differs by phone brand).
  • What ads never receive: your game progress, scores or settings. These stay on your device.
  • More information: Unity Privacy Policy and Unity Ads & cookies.

5. Technology and Third-Party Components

  • Unity Engine – the game engine the Game is built with. Game logic runs locally on your device.
  • Unity Ads SDK – shows ads and sends and receives data over the Internet (Section 4).

6. How We Use and Share Information

Game data on your device is used only to save your progress and settings. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone. Advertising data is handled by Unity as described in Section 4.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising identifiers may be shared with Unity Ads (the ad network) and advertisers that receive ad delivery information through the network. We do not otherwise share information with third parties. If this changes, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before releasing that version.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data created by the Game stays on your device. To delete it, open Phone Settings → Apps → Puzzle Game → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the Game. We keep no server-side copy of your gameplay data. Advertising data held by Unity is retained according to Unity's retention schedule (see Unity's privacy policy).

8. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child under 13 is using the Game, or that a child's data has been collected, please contact us (Section 14) and we will take appropriate action.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Advertising choices.
