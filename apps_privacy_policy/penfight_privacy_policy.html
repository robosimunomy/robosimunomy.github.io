1. About the Game

Pen Fight (কলম খেলা) is a free, single-player Android game about flicking pen lids across a school desk — the classic classroom pen fight. It is supported by advertising. The Game has no login, no online multiplayer and no server of ours that receives your data. The physics, the bot opponent, the 20-level campaign, the save file and the English/Bangla interface all run entirely on your device, and the Game can be played offline.

2. Information We Collect and Process

2.1 What we do NOT collect

We do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone data.

2.2 Information stored on your device only

DataWhat happens to it
Current level, highest level cleared, stars, total score, coins, unlocked pens and achievementsSaved in the Game's private storage on your phone (Android SharedPreferences) so you can continue where you stopped. Never uploaded.
Sound, music, haptics, aim guide, motion and language settingsSaved in the Game's private storage on your phone. Used only to remember your choices.
Ad break counters (how many matches you have started since the last interstitial)Stored on your device only, so the Game can show an interstitial every fifth match you start. Nothing else is recorded.

This data is removed when you clear the Game's data or uninstall it. On some phones Android may also back it up to your Google account; that backup is controlled by Android's own backup settings, not by us.

2.3 Information collected by our advertising partner

Because the Game shows ads, Google AdMob (a third-party service) may collect and process certain data from your device when ads are requested and shown. This is described in Section 4. We do not receive this data ourselves.

Location, camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, calendar, storage, or access to your photo library. The Game does not run in the background to collect data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob (app id ca-app-pub-4342224983673116~6312350724) to show these ads:

  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads – shown only at natural break points: after you win a match or clear a level, on the fifth match you start, and when you unlock a pen you can afford in the shop. Losing, drawing or quitting a match never shows one, and the break has a visible close button once it has run its ten seconds.
  • Rewarded video ads – completely optional and shown only when you choose to watch one: in the shop, on a pen you cannot afford, the Game offers "Watch ad · +30 coins". The reward is credited only after the video is rewarded, and the amount (30 coins) is set by the Game, not by the ad.
  • Data Google and its partners may collect: your advertising ID (AAID/GAID), IP address (which gives an approximate location at country/region level), device model, operating-system version, language, app information, ad interactions (views and taps), and diagnostic or performance data. They use this to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and abuse, and pay ad revenue.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads: depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID, or non-personalised.
  • Your consent choices: in regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), Google's consent tools apply. You can change or withdraw your choice through Google's ads controls or through your device settings at any time.
  • Control of your advertising ID: on most Android phones you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Phone Settings → Google → Ads (the path differs by phone brand).
  • What ads never receive: your game progress, stars, coins, unlocked pens or settings. These stay on your device, and the ad units never receive them.
  • No ads from us during play: there are no banner ads, no pop-ups outside these three placements, and nothing is charged — there are no in-app purchases in the Game at all.
  • More information: Google Privacy Policy, About Google ads & privacy and Google Ads Settings.

5. Technology and Third-Party Components

  • Android / AndroidX / Material Components – the platform and UI libraries the Game is built with. Everything runs locally on your device.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK – shows ads and sends and receives data over the Internet (Section 4).
  • Self-written game engine – the collision and physics engine, the bot AI, the scoring and the save system were written from scratch for this Game. No analytics, crash-reporting, social or tracking SDK is included.

6. How We Use and Share Information

Game data on your device is used only to save your progress and settings. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone. Advertising data is handled by Google as described in Section 4.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising identifiers may be shared with Google AdMob (the ad network) and advertisers that receive ad delivery information through the network. We do not otherwise share information with third parties. If this changes, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before releasing that version.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data created by the Game stays on your device. To delete it, open Phone Settings → Apps → Pen Fight → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the Game. We keep no server-side copy of your gameplay data. Advertising data held by Google is retained according to Google's retention schedule (see Google's privacy policy).
