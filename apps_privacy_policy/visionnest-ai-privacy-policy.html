1. About the App

VisionNest AI is an educational and informational tool that uses on-device artificial-intelligence models to analyse images. It includes plant leaf disease detection for several crops and garden plants, additional image-classification tools, live camera object detection (YOLO-family and EfficientNet models), text and voice read-out of results, and general care tips for some results. The App is free and is supported by advertising.

2. Information We Collect and Process

2.1 What we do NOT collect

We do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. There is no login. Your photos, camera frames, AI results, and "last prediction" are not sent to us.

2.2 Information processed on your device only

DataWhat happens to it
Photos you take with the App's camera buttonSaved briefly as a temporary file in the App's private folder, loaded into memory for analysis, and then deleted. Never uploaded.
Images you choose from your galleryYou pick one image with Android's built-in picker. The App receives only that image, uses it for the analysis, and does not copy or upload it.
Live camera frames (Live Detection)Processed in real time in memory to draw detection boxes. Not recorded, saved, or uploaded.
Your "Voice" on/off choiceSaved in the App's private storage on your phone (More page). Used only to decide whether the prediction result is read aloud.
Your "Show ads" on/off choiceSaved in the App's private storage on your phone (More page). Used only to decide whether ads are requested and shown.
Your last prediction (plant name, result, confidence %)Saved in the App's private storage so the "Last Prediction" card can show it. It stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

2.3 Information collected by our advertising partner

Because the App shows ads, Google AdMob (a third-party service) may collect and process certain data from your device when ads are requested and shown. This is described in Section 5. We do not receive this data ourselves, and it never includes your photos or scan results.

2.4 Backup

The App turns off Android app backup, so its local data is not included in cloud backups of your phone.

4. Technology and Third-Party Components

  • LiteRT / TensorFlow Lite (Google) – runs the AI models on your device. Works locally.
  • CameraX (Android Jetpack) – shows the camera preview and provides frames to the model. Works locally.
  • Android Text-to-Speech – reads results aloud (you can turn this off with the Voice switch in the More page) using the speech engine already installed on your phone. That engine is governed by its own provider's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK – shows ads. Sends and receives data over the Internet (Section 5).
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) – shows the consent message where the law requires one.

5. Advertising

The App shows banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob.

  • Data Google and its partners may collect: your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and operating-system information, app information, ad interactions (views and taps), and diagnostic or performance data. They use this to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure and working.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads: depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised based on your interests, or non-personalised (based on general context such as the App content and your approximate area).
  • Your consent choices: in regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent message from Google before any ad is requested. If you decline, no ad is requested. If your region requires it, you can change your choice later from the menu: Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Control of your advertising ID: on most Android phones you can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalisation in Phone Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path differs by phone brand).
  • When ads appear: a small banner is shown at the bottom of the Home and Premium pages. A full-screen ad can appear occasionally, only after you finish a scan or detection and leave that screen (for example, when you press Back on the Live Detection screen or return to a main page). It is never shown when the App opens, never while the camera is running, and never more than once after every two finished tasks. Full-screen ads can be closed, and you can turn all ads off in the More page.
  • Ads on/off switch: the More page has a Show ads switch. Turn it off and the App stops requesting and showing ads, and does not start the ad service or its consent form. You can turn it on again at any time. Turning ads off does not change how scanning works. The choice is stored only on your phone.
  • What ads never receive: your photos, camera images, scan results, or the "last prediction". These stay on your device.
  • More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not place ads that are meant for children, and the App is intended for adults.

They are not used for advertising, profiling, or AI training. Advertising data is handled by Google as described in Section 5.

10. Accuracy, Limitations, and Important Disclaimer

Please read carefully. The AI results in VisionNest AI are estimates. They are not 100% accurate and can be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.
  1. Not 100% accurate. The models are trained on limited sets of images. They can miss a problem, report a problem that does not exist, or choose the wrong class. A high confidence percentage does not guarantee the result is correct.
  2. Not professional advice. Results, confidence scores, and care tips are for general information and education only. They are not a substitute for the judgement of an agronomist, agriculture officer, plant pathologist, doctor, veterinarian, engineer, disaster-management authority, or other qualified specialist.
  3. Consult a specialist before acting. Before you apply any pesticide, fungicide, fertiliser or other treatment, remove or destroy plants or crops, make a purchase, or take any decision based on the App, you must consult a qualified local specialist. Following the App alone may not work and may cause crop loss, plant damage, financial loss, or other harm.
  4. Chemicals and safety. The App does not recommend specific chemical products or doses. Always follow the product label and local regulations, and use proper protection.
  5. Many things affect results. Poor or uneven light, blur, shadows, a dirty lens, distance, cluttered background, several leaves in one frame, unusual angle, plants or diseases the model was not trained on, early-stage or mixed infections, nutrient deficiency or pest damage that looks like disease, and low-end phone performance can all reduce accuracy.
  6. Limited coverage. The App supports only the plants and classes included in each model. A disease or object that is not in the model may be matched to the closest known class or not detected at all. Using the wrong model for a picture (for example a rice leaf in the tomato model) gives wrong results.
  7. Live detection. Real-time object detection (YOLO / EfficientNet models) is approximate. Boxes and labels may be missing, wrong, or flicker, and speed depends on your device. Do not use it for safety-critical, security, navigation, or emergency decisions.
  8. Not a medical device. Any tool that analyses body-related or other non-plant images is for general demonstration and learning only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and must not replace a doctor's advice. If you have a health concern, see a qualified medical professional.
  9. Disaster, colour, and similar tools. These are informational and can be wrong. Never rely on them for emergency, evacuation, or safety decisions. Follow official authorities.
  10. Care tips. General tips shown after a result are generic and not tailored to your soil, climate, crop stage, or local rules.
  11. Voice read-out. Text-to-speech depends on your phone's speech engine and language support and may be mispronounced or unavailable.
  12. Availability. The App is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted or error-free operation. Features and models may change, and some may not work on every device or Android version.
