This Privacy Policy explains how the VisionNest AI Android app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information, which device permission it asks for and why, and the important limits of its AI results. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Short summary
  • The App works offline. It does not use the Internet permission and does not upload your photos, camera images, or results to us or to anyone else.
  • No account or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising ID.
  • The App asks for one permission only: Camera, to take photos and run live detection. It does not ask for storage, photo-library, location, microphone, or contacts permission.

1. About the App

VisionNest AI is an educational and informational tool that uses on-device artificial-intelligence models to analyse images. It includes plant leaf disease detection for several crops and garden plants, additional image-classification tools, live camera object detection (YOLO-family and EfficientNet models), text and voice read-out of results, and general care tips for some results.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, rent, or share any personal information. The App has no login, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and no server that receives your data. The App does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your images or results out of your device.

2.2 Information processed on your device only

DataWhat happens to it
Photos you take with the App's camera buttonThe photo is saved briefly as a temporary file in the App's private folder, loaded into memory for analysis, and then deleted. It is never uploaded.
Images you choose from your galleryYou pick one image with Android's built-in picker. The App receives only that image, uses it for the analysis, and does not copy or upload it.
Live camera frames (Live Detection)Processed in real time in memory to draw detection boxes. They are not recorded, saved, or uploaded.
Your last prediction (plant name, result, confidence %)Saved in the App's private storage so the "Last Prediction" card can show it. It stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

2.3 Backup

The App turns off Android app backup, so its local data is not included in cloud backups of your phone.

3. Permission Used and Why

PermissionPurpose in the AppWhen it is askedIf you deny it
Camera
CAMERA		 To take a photo of a leaf or object for analysis, and to show the live camera preview for real-time detection. Only when you tap the Camera button or open a Live Detection model. You cannot take photos or use Live Detection. You can still analyse images from your gallery.

You can turn this permission off at any time in Phone Settings → Apps → VisionNest AI → Permissions.

Choosing images from your gallery

The App does not request storage, photos, or media permission. When you tap Gallery, Android's built-in picker opens and the App receives only the single image you select. It cannot browse or read the rest of your photos or files.

Permissions the App does NOT use

Internet, location, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, calendar, storage, or access to your whole photo library. The App does not run in the background to collect data.

4. Technology and Third-Party Components

The App uses these components, which all run locally on your phone and do not send your data anywhere through the App:

  • LiteRT / TensorFlow Lite (Google) – runs the AI models on your device.
  • CameraX (Android Jetpack) – shows the camera preview and provides frames to the model.
  • Android Text-to-Speech – reads results aloud using the speech engine already installed on your phone (for example Google Text-to-Speech). That engine is part of your device and is governed by its own provider's privacy policy.
  • Android system camera and image picker – used to take and choose photos.

5. How We Use Information

Images and results are used only to (a) run the AI analysis you requested, (b) show you the result and related care tips, and (c) show your last prediction on the home screen. They are not used for advertising, profiling, AI training, or any other purpose.

6. Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not receive it. The App contains no ad networks, analytics, or crash-reporting services. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before releasing that version.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stays on your device. To delete it, open Phone Settings → Apps → VisionNest AI → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. Because we hold no copy, we have no data to delete on our side. Pictures in your own gallery are not changed or removed by the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects nothing, no child's data is collected. Younger users should use the App only with a parent or guardian.

9. Accuracy, Limitations, and Important Disclaimer

Please read carefully. The AI results in VisionNest AI are estimates. They are not 100% accurate and can be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.
  1. Not 100% accurate. The models are trained on limited sets of images. They can miss a problem, report a problem that does not exist, or choose the wrong class. A high confidence percentage does not guarantee the result is correct.
  2. Not professional advice. Results, confidence scores, and care tips are for general information and education only. They are not a substitute for the judgement of an agronomist, agriculture officer, plant pathologist, doctor, veterinarian, engineer, disaster-management authority, or other qualified specialist.
  3. Consult a specialist before acting. Before you apply any pesticide, fungicide, fertiliser or other treatment, remove or destroy plants or crops, make a purchase, or take any decision based on the App, you must consult a qualified local specialist. Following the App alone may not work and may cause crop loss, plant damage, financial loss, or other harm.
  4. Chemicals and safety. The App does not recommend specific chemical products or doses. Always follow the product label and local regulations, and use proper protection.
  5. Many things affect results. Poor or uneven light, blur, shadows, a dirty lens, distance, cluttered background, several leaves in one frame, unusual angle, plants or diseases the model was not trained on, early-stage or mixed infections, nutrient deficiency or pest damage that looks like disease, and low-end phone performance can all reduce accuracy.
  6. Limited coverage. The App supports only the plants and classes included in each model. A disease or object that is not in the model may be matched to the closest known class or not detected at all. Using the wrong model for a picture (for example a rice leaf in the tomato model) gives wrong results.
  7. Live detection. Real-time object detection (YOLO / EfficientNet models) is approximate. Boxes and labels may be missing, wrong, or flicker, and speed depends on your device. Do not use it for safety-critical, security, navigation, or emergency decisions.
  8. Not a medical device. Any tool that analyses body-related or other non-plant images is for general demonstration and learning only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and must not replace a doctor's advice. If you have a health concern, see a qualified medical professional.
  9. Disaster, colour, and similar tools. These are informational and can be wrong. Never rely on them for emergency, evacuation, or safety decisions. Follow official authorities.
  10. Care tips. General tips shown after a result are generic and not tailored to your soil, climate, crop stage, or local rules.
  11. Voice read-out. Text-to-speech depends on your phone's speech engine and language support and may be mispronounced or unavailable.
  12. Availability. The App is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted or error-free operation. Features and models may change, and some may not work on every device or Android version.
