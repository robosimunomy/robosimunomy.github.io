VisionNest AI – Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Developer: RoboSimunomy  |  Effective date: October 3, 2026  |  Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VisionNest AI Android app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information, which device permissions it asks for and why, and the important limitations of its AI results. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Short summary
  • The App works offline. It does not request the Internet permission and does not upload your photos, camera frames, or results to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising ID.
  • Camera and photo access are used only to run the AI model on your device, when you ask for it.
  • The AI results can be wrong. They are not professional advice. Always confirm with a qualified specialist before taking action. See Section 8.

1. About the App

VisionNest AI is an educational and informational tool that uses on-device artificial-intelligence models (TensorFlow Lite) to analyse images. It includes plant leaf disease detection for several crops and garden plants, additional image-classification tools, live camera object detection using YOLO-family and EfficientNet models, text and voice read-out of results, and general care tips for some results.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, rent, or share any personal information. The App has no sign-up, no login, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no server that receives your data. The App does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your images or results out of your device.

2.2 Information processed on your device only

DataWhat happens to it
Photos you take or choose from your galleryAnalysed locally by the AI model to produce a result. Photos taken inside the App may be saved as temporary files in the App's own private storage folder. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
Live camera frames (Live Detection)Processed in real time in memory to draw detection boxes. Frames are not recorded or saved unless you capture a photo.
Your last prediction (plant name, result, confidence %)Saved in the App's private storage so the "Last Prediction" card can show it. It stays on your device and is removed when you clear App data or uninstall.

2.3 Device backup

Android's automatic backup feature may include the App's small local settings (such as the last prediction) in your own Google account backup, depending on your phone settings. This is controlled by your device and Google, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone settings.

The App asks for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can deny or withdraw any permission in Phone Settings → Apps → VisionNest AI → Permissions. If you deny a permission, only the related feature stops working.

Permissions the App does NOT use

The App does not access your location, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, calendar, files outside of what you select, or the Internet. It does not run in the background to collect data.

Text-to-speech

The App may read results aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example, Google Text-to-Speech). This needs no extra permission. The engine is part of your device and is governed by its own provider's privacy policy.

4. How We Use Information

Images and results are used only to (a) run the AI analysis you requested, (b) show you the result and the related care tips, and (c) show your last prediction on the home screen. They are not used for advertising, profiling, model training, or any other purpose.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not receive it. The App contains no advertising networks, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy before releasing the change, and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated as well.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stays on your device. To delete it, open Phone Settings → Apps → VisionNest AI → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. Since we hold no copy, we have no data to delete on our side. Pictures in your own gallery are not removed by the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences, including farmers, gardeners, students, and hobbyists. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects nothing, no child's data is collected. Children should use the App with a parent or guardian.

8. Accuracy, Limitations, and Important Disclaimer

Please read carefully. The AI results in VisionNest AI are estimates. They are not 100% accurate and can be incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.
  1. Not 100% accurate. The models are trained on limited sets of images. They can miss a problem (false negative), report a problem that does not exist (false positive), or choose the wrong class. A high confidence percentage does not guarantee the result is correct.
  2. Not professional advice. Results, confidence scores, and care tips are for general information and education only. They are not a substitute for the judgement of an agronomist, agriculture officer, plant pathologist, veterinarian, doctor, engineer, disaster-management authority, or other qualified specialist.
  3. Consult a specialist before acting. Before you apply any pesticide, fungicide, fertiliser, or other treatment, remove or destroy plants or crops, make a purchase, or take any decision based on the App, you must consult a qualified local specialist. Following the App alone may not work and may cause crop loss, plant damage, financial loss, or other harm.
  4. Chemicals and safety. The App does not recommend specific chemical products or doses. Always follow the product label and local regulations, and wear proper protection.
  5. Many things affect results. Poor or uneven light, blur, shadows, dirty lens, distance, cluttered background, multiple leaves in one frame, unusual leaf angle, plants or diseases the model was not trained on, early-stage or mixed infections, nutrient deficiency or pest damage that looks like disease, and low-end phone performance can all reduce accuracy.
  6. Limited coverage. The App supports only the plants and classes included in each model. A disease or object that is not in the model will be wrongly matched to the closest known class or not detected at all.
  7. Live detection. Real-time object detection (YOLO / EfficientNet models) is approximate. Boxes and labels may be missing, wrong, or flicker, and speed depends on your device. Do not use it for safety-critical, security, navigation, or emergency decisions.
  8. Health-related and other classification tools. Any tool that analyses body-related or other non-plant images is for general demonstration and learning only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and must not replace a doctor's advice. If you have a health concern, see a qualified medical professional.
  9. Disaster, colour, and similar tools. These are informational and can be wrong. Never rely on them for emergency, evacuation, or safety decisions. Follow official authorities.
  10. Care tips. General tips shown after a result are generic and not tailored to your soil, climate, crop stage, or local rules.
  11. Voice read-out. The text-to-speech output depends on your phone's speech engine and language support and may be mispronounced or unavailable.
  12. Availability. The App is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted or error-free operation. Features and models may change, and some may not work on every device or Android version.
